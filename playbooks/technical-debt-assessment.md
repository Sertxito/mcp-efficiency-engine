# Technical debt assessment

## Scope
Use to establish and prioritize maintenance liabilities in a bounded system.
This is not a maturity score or a monetary valuation.

## Procedure
1. Define scope, debt categories, decision purpose, and evidence access.
2. Record debt candidates with source links, impact, confidence, and uncertainty.
3. Separate structural evidence from tool warnings and hypotheses.
4. Prioritize against supplied business criteria; document the method.
5. Assign owner and review trigger to accepted debt where known.

## Outputs
A source-linked register, priority rationale, accepted items, unknowns, and
bounded next actions.

## Safety boundaries
Do not claim cost-of-delay or risk values without a stated method. Do not
initiate broad remediation without approval and behavior-preserving checks.
