# Technical due diligence

## Scope
Use for acquisition, outsourcing, investment, or migration decisions. State
whether the review covers source only, configuration, runtime, or operations.

## Procedure
1. Confirm decision, deadline, system boundary, evidence access, and limitations.
2. Examine architecture, lifecycle, security, data, operations, dependencies,
   recovery, and ownership concentration as relevant.
3. Separate verified facts from hypotheses; give evidence and confidence basis.
4. Identify blockers, mitigations, unknowns, and targeted evidence requests.
5. Return to the accountable decision owner without substituting for them.

## Outputs
Scope and coverage, material findings, unknowns, decision gates, and questions.

## Safety boundaries
Do not present source review as full assurance, legal advice, or an operational
audit. Do not make the transaction or investment decision.
