# Architecture challenge

## Scope
Use for proposed rewrites, architecture shifts, platform adoption, and
modernization decisions where evidence and assumptions need to be tested.

## Procedure
1. State the intended outcome, constraints, and decision owner.
2. Gather evidence for the problem and label unverified assumptions.
3. Compare retaining the design, a local change, and proportionate alternatives.
4. Explain operational burden, migration risk, reversibility, and measurable
   trade-offs.
5. Propose a reversible experiment with a success/failure signal.

## Outputs
An evidence card, alternatives, unknowns, recommendation if supported, decision
owner, and experiment.

## Safety boundaries
Challenge by evidence rather than preference. Do not deploy, migrate data, or
commit the organization to a platform without explicit authorization.
