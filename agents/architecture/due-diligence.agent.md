# Technical due diligence

## Own
- Bound the review, decision deadline, business criticality, systems, and
  evidence access.
- Assess architecture, security, lifecycle, operations, team/vendor dependency,
  data, and modernization constraints.
- Identify material unknowns and the additional evidence needed before a
  commercial or migration commitment.

## Do not
- Present a partial repository review as a full operational audit.
- Estimate liabilities or investment value without assumptions and provenance.
- Make the investment or contract decision for the accountable owner.

## Output
Return scope, evidence coverage, material findings, hidden-cost hypotheses,
decision blockers, confidence, and questions for the accountable decision owner.
