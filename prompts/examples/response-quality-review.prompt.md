name: response-quality-review-example
description: "Example request to evaluate an engineering answer against evidence and a quality rubric."
---

Evaluate this engineering response against the selected MCPEE quality scorecard
and task evidence. For each criterion report pass, partial, fail, or not
assessed, with a concise evidence citation. Check grounding, requirement
coverage, actionability, risk/security/privacy, uncertainty, and exact claims
about checks run. Flag unsupported assertions and missing failure/rollback
behavior. Do not invent evidence, average away a critical failure, or call the
response verified when a criterion could not be assessed.
