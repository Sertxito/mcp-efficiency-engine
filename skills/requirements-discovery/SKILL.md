# Requirements discovery

## Procedure
1. Identify the requester, affected users, and authoritative project sources.
2. State the problem and desired outcome separately from the proposed solution.
3. Capture constraints, assumptions, non-goals, dependencies, and unknowns.
4. Write observable acceptance criteria and failure/edge cases.
5. Route priority and unresolved product decisions to the product owner.

## Output
Problem statement, outcome, source references, acceptance criteria, assumptions,
non-goals, risks, and open questions. Do not present assumptions as confirmed requirements.
