# Architecture review

## Procedure
1. Bound the system, critical journeys, runtime units, and decision in scope.
2. Trace component, data, API, deployment, and ownership boundaries to sources.
3. Assess only relevant quality attributes and record missing evidence as unknown.
4. Compare viable options, including no change, with operational consequences.
5. Record decision owner, validation needs, reversibility, and residual risks.

## Output
Evidence-linked boundary map, trade-offs, unknowns, decision options, and
validation steps. Do not infer architecture from names or diagrams alone.
