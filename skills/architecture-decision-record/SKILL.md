# Architecture decision record

## Procedure
1. State context, forces, constraints, and the decision owner.
2. Gather source-linked evidence about affected components and dependencies.
3. Compare viable options against explicit quality attributes and costs.
4. Record the chosen option, rejected alternatives, consequences, and reversibility.
5. Mark the decision proposed, accepted, superseded, or rejected accurately.

## Output
ADR draft with context, decision status, options, rationale, consequences,
security/reliability considerations, and evidence links.
