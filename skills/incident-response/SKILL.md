# Incident response

## Procedure
1. Establish incident scope, impact, time window, and response owner.
2. Collect current telemetry and distinguish facts from hypotheses.
3. Prioritize containment and reversible mitigations; obtain action-specific approval.
4. Verify recovery against user-facing signals and monitor for recurrence.
5. Preserve a timeline and create a blameless review with owned follow-ups.

## Output
Impact, evidence/timeline, hypotheses, approved actions and results, recovery
criteria, unresolved risks, and follow-up tasks. Do not perform destructive changes
or external communications without authorization.
