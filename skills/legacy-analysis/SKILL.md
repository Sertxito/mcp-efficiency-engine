# Legacy analysis

## Procedure
1. Identify business-critical journeys, owners, runtimes, deployment paths, and
   evidence limits.
2. Trace entry points, data ownership, integrations, batch jobs, and recovery
   paths from source and runtime evidence.
3. Record support lifecycle, build/release fragility, knowledge concentration,
   and critical dependencies as facts or hypotheses.
4. Separate immediate stabilization from modernization options; preserve
   behavior until compatibility is understood.
5. Define a prioritized next-evidence and verification plan with owners.

## Output
Current-state facts, unknowns, continuity risks, stabilization priorities, and
incremental options. Do not infer that old technology must be rewritten.
