# Technical due diligence

## Procedure
1. Confirm decision, deadline, system boundary, evidence access, and coverage
   limitations.
2. Inventory architecture, lifecycle, security, operations, data, dependencies,
   owners, and vendor/team concentration.
3. Distinguish verified liabilities from hypotheses and quantify only from
   sourced assumptions.
4. Identify commitment blockers, mitigations, and evidence needed before the
   decision.
5. Return findings and questions to the accountable business/technical owner.

## Output
Scope, evidence register, material risks, unknowns, cost hypotheses, and
decision gates. Do not make the investment decision or claim complete assurance.
