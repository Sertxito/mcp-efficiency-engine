# Close loop and learn

**Trigger:** A feature, release, incident, or evaluation has completed with evidence.

**Lead roles:** Team lead; QA lead; technical writer.

## Stages
1. Compare observed outcome with acceptance criteria and expected service behavior.
2. Record validation, deployment, observability, and user-feedback evidence.
3. Update affected documentation and operational runbooks from verified behavior.
4. Identify recurring failure patterns and propose a skill/workflow/policy improvement.
5. Obtain owner approval before persisting durable lessons or changing shared process.

## Outputs
Completion evidence, documentation changes, measured follow-ups, and reviewable
learning proposals. Do not convert a single anecdote into a universal rule.
