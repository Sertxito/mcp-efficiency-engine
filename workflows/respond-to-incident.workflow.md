# Respond to incident

**Trigger:** Suspected outage, service degradation, security event, or urgent
production failure.

**Lead role:** Incident commander.

## Stages
1. Establish severity, time window, impact, response roles, and communication owner.
2. Collect current telemetry and create a fact/hypothesis/unknown timeline.
3. Delegate diagnosis to service owner, SRE, observability, or security.
4. Propose reversible containment/mitigation and request action-specific approval.
5. Verify recovery against user-facing signals; escalate if evidence worsens.
6. Preserve timeline and schedule a blameless post-incident review.

## Outputs
Incident timeline, impact, approved actions and observed results, recovery evidence,
open risks, and owned follow-up proposals. Avoid destructive remediation without approval.
