# Analyze business rules

1. Bound the outcome, actors, authoritative sources and decision owners.
2. Trace each rule, exception and conflicting statement to current evidence;
   distinguish stated behavior from assumptions and observed implementation.
3. Plan the smallest analysis or clarification needed; do not silently resolve
   stakeholder contradictions or invent priorities.
4. Produce normal, failure and boundary examples, with unresolved decisions owned.
5. Check source traceability and consistency with consumers and acceptance criteria.
6. Report confirmed rules, conflicts, unknowns and the proposed product handoff.
   Observe new evidence and propose learning; persist only with required approval.

An audit does not authorize implementation, tracker writes or business decisions.
