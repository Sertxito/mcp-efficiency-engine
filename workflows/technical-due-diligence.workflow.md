# Technical due diligence

1. Confirm decision, deadline, scope, evidence access, and limits of coverage.
2. Assess architecture, lifecycle, security, data, operations, dependencies,
   recoverability, and team/vendor concentration.
3. Separate verified material findings from untested hypotheses and unknowns.
4. Identify commitment blockers, mitigations, and additional evidence needed.
5. Deliver findings and questions to the accountable decision owner.

Do not present a source-only review as an operational audit or make the
investment/contract decision.
