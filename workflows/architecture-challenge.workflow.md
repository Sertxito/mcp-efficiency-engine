# Challenge architecture

1. Restate the proposed design, motivating evidence, desired outcome, and
   constraints.
2. Trace affected boundaries and validate the assumptions on which the proposal
   depends.
3. Compare no change, a local fix, and proportionate structural alternatives.
4. Explain operational burden, reversibility, migration risk, and measurable
   trade-offs.
5. Identify the smallest experiment and accountable decision owner.

Return a challenge, not a veto. Do not deploy or make the decision for its owner.
