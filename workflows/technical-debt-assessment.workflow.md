# Assess technical debt

1. Define system boundary, debt categories, decision purpose, and evidence limits.
2. Inventory candidate debt with source links and fact/hypothesis status.
3. Describe demonstrated impact, confidence, dependencies, and uncertainty.
4. Prioritize using supplied business criteria; avoid unsupported precision.
5. Return an owned register, accepted-debt review triggers, and bounded next steps.

Do not equate a smell or scanner warning with material business risk.
