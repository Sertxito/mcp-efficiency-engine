# AI platform evidence checklist

Map model and version, retrieval sources and freshness, access trimming, tool
permissions, trust boundaries, user/session isolation, evaluation coverage,
privacy/retention, failure fallback, latency, cost, and audit signals. For each
claim, record evidence, method, date, and known limitations. Test normal,
ambiguous, unavailable-source/tool, authorization, and adversarial inputs.

This checklist does not certify safety, grounding, or maturity.
