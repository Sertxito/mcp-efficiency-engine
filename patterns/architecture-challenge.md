# Architecture challenge evidence card

## Capture
- Proposed change and intended outcome.
- Evidence motivating the proposal and assumptions that remain unverified.
- Existing boundary and constraint sources.
- No-change, local-fix, and proportionate structural alternatives.
- Operational burden, reversibility, migration risk, and measurable trade-offs.
- Smallest experiment and accountable decision owner.

Treat this as decision support, not a veto. Distinguish facts from hypotheses.
