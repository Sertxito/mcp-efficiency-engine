# Due diligence finding

## Record each material finding
- Decision relevance and bounded review scope.
- Source evidence and coverage limitation.
- Verified fact, hypothesis, or unknown; confidence and impact basis.
- Owner, mitigation, commitment blocker, and evidence request.
- Follow-up question for the accountable decision owner.
- Root-cause evidence, blast radius and incremental remediation; keep severity,
  confidence, effort/value assumptions and decision priority distinct.
- Exposure/drift, operational ownership, cost accountability and tested
  recovery evidence when relevant to a project takeover.

Do not make investment, contract, or assurance claims beyond the reviewed scope.
